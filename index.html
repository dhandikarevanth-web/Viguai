Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about VigilAI

How does VigilAI integrate with existing CCTV systems?

VigilAI works with virtually all IP camera systems and DVR/NVR setups. Our platform connects via standard protocols (RTSP, ONVIF) without requiring hardware replacements. Our technical team handles the entire integration process, typically completing setup within 24-48 hours.

Is VigilAI GDPR and CCPA compliant?

Yes, VigilAI is fully compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and other major privacy regulations. We implement privacy-by-design principles, including data encryption, anonymization options, access controls, and comprehensive audit trails. Our platform includes built-in compliance tools and documentation.

What is the accuracy rate of threat detection?

Our AI maintains a 99.7% accuracy rate in threat detection, with a false positive rate under 0.5%. The system continuously learns and improves based on your specific environment. During the calibration period, our AI adapts to your unique security requirements and operational patterns.

How quickly can we deploy VigilAI?

Most deployments are completed within 48-72 hours. For smaller installations (under 20 cameras), we can often complete setup within 24 hours. Enterprise deployments with hundreds of cameras typically take 1-2 weeks, including full training and calibration.

What kind of support do you offer?

We provide 24/7 technical support for all Professional and Enterprise plans. Support includes phone, email, and live chat channels. Enterprise clients receive a dedicated account manager and priority response times. We also offer comprehensive training programs and regular system optimization reviews.
